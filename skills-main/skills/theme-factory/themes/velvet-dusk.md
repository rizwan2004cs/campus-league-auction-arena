# Velvet Dusk

A sophisticated pastel theme that blends soft lavender, blush pink, powder blue, and warm peach into a harmonious gradient wash — never a single dominant color but a seamless iridescent mist.

## Color Palette

- **Lavender Mist**: `#e0d4fc` - Soft violet tint for glass surfaces and accents
- **Powder Blue**: `#cee9ff` - Cool pastel blue for secondary highlights  
- **Rose Quartz**: `#ffd8ec` - Warm blush for gradient warmth and soft accents
- **Peach Cream**: `#ffe4cc` - Warm amber undertone for depth
- **Ink**: `#2d2640` - Deep plum-charcoal for text, readable on all pastels

## Typography

- **Headers**: Bricolage Grotesque — characterful geometric grotesque with optical sizing
- **Body Text**: Plus Jakarta Sans — refined humanist sans with excellent legibility

## Best Used For

Premium dashboards, auction platforms, creative management tools, modern SaaS applications with a luxurious, approachable aesthetic.
